Blast Ball

Privacy Policy

Blast Ball is published by JHR Studios. This Privacy Policy explains how the Android game handles data when you play and use its online features.

Data we use

Blast Ball uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played and to improve game balance and performance. Analytics receives an identifier for your app installation, device and operating system information, approximate location derived from your IP address, and session and usage events. Gameplay events can include stage progress, scores, balls launched, hits and blocks broken, virtual coins earned and spent, skins used, and use of controls such as speed and retry.

Blast Ball stores game progress and preferences on your device so you can continue playing.

Online leaderboard

Blast Ball uses Google Play Games Services to operate its online leaderboard. When you use the leaderboard, Google Play Games may process your player ID, profile name, avatar, and scores. Your Play Games settings affect who can see your profile and gameplay data. Google Play Games Services may also collect usage and diagnostic data to improve its stability.

Google services and data sharing

Google handles data through Google Analytics for Firebase, BigQuery, and Google Play Games Services. Google Analytics support representatives can access Analytics data when needed to resolve technical issues. We export Analytics events to Google Cloud BigQuery daily for gameplay and balance analysis. The BigQuery dataset is located in the United States.

For more information, see Google Analytics for Firebase data disclosure and Google Play Games Services data disclosure.

Retention and security

Our Google Analytics property is configured to retain event data for two months and user-level data for 14 months. New activity resets the retention period for the associated user identifier.

BigQuery export tables are configured to expire 60 days after creation. After expiration, data may remain temporarily available through BigQuery's recovery mechanisms.

Google Analytics for Firebase and Google Play Games Services encrypt data in transit.

Your choices and contact

You can manage or delete Blast Ball data held by Google Play Games through your Google Play Games profile settings. For privacy inquiries, contact JHR Studios at jhrstudio.testes@gmail.com.

Updates to this policy

We may update this policy when our data practices change. The current version will be available on this page.
