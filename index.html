Data the game handles

Blast Ball uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how people use the game and to improve it. The data may include app installation identifiers, device and operating system information, approximate location derived from the IP address, play sessions, and usage events.

Gameplay events may include stage progress, scores, balls launched, hits and blocks broken, virtual coins earned and spent, skins used, and use of controls such as speed and retry.

Your progress and preferences may also be stored on your device so you can continue playing.

Online leaderboard and Google Play Games

The online leaderboard uses Google Play Games Services. When you use this feature, the service may process your player ID, profile name, avatar, and scores. Your Google Play Games settings determine who can see your profile and gameplay data. The service may also collect diagnostic and usage data to improve its stability.

Learn more about how these services handle data in the documentation for Google Play Games Services and Google Analytics for Firebase.
